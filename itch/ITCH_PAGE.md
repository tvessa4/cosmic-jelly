## Description (вставить в поле Description)

**Cosmic Jelly** is a cozy take on 2048 set in a night sky full of glowing jelly cubes.

🪐 **How to play** — swipe (or use the arrow keys / WASD) to slide every tile. Two equal tiles merge into one. Reach **2048** — and keep going!

✨ **Features**
- Jelly tiles that wobble when you tap them
- Glip, a little alien slime who cheers your combos and gets nervous when the board is full
- 17 tile colors **plus a unique symbol on every tile** — color-blind friendly
- Boosters: Undo, Hammer, Shuffle — buy more with coins
- Daily reward streak and a **Daily Challenge** with the same tiles for everyone
- 15 achievements, autosave, original synthesized sounds and music

🇷🇺 **По-русски:** уютная 2048 в космосе. Свайпай, соединяй одинаковые желейные плитки, собери 2048 и радуй маскота Глипа. Ежедневные награды, испытание дня, достижения, бустеры.
